# Compliance Boundaries

Phase 1 operates under strict compliance boundaries to avoid complex regulatory overhead:

1. **No Political Campaigns**: Do not launch campaigns involving electoral candidates, ballot measures, or political contributions.
2. **No Investments/Securities**: Do not offer financial profit-sharing, lotteries, or games of chance.
3. **No Tax-Deductible Promises**: Do not describe a merchandise purchase as a charitable donation unless legally cleared.
4. **Restricted Products**: Standard restricted commerce rules apply (no illicit goods).
5. **Demonstration Focus**: The initial deployment will feature a fictional "Neighborhood Lot Decision" to safely prove the product mechanics.
